## Current revision protocol rollout

Revision protocol two was introduced in Order 13, Payment 12 and POS 10 with compatibility **0.26.0**. Current coordinated deployment uses Order 16 / Payment 15 / POS 13 at **0.29.0**, including the seal/journal extension above. Reporting remains schema 20; Authorization remains 10. Apply migrations before enabling current cutoff binaries; missing revision tables fail unavailable. Pause cutoff commands during the coordinated source/Reporting/POS/BFF/portal upgrade. Source protocols are additive to existing routes; old manifests are readable but cannot become Ready, so refresh using a new operation ID. Ordinary draft/preparation and older acceptance modes retain their separate schema targets.

The new migrations add two tables per owner: an immutable source epoch and Restaurant/branch revision rows. Source mutation triggers need the service runtime role's existing SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE privileges on the revision table and SELECT on epoch/owning parents. Existing bootstrap default grants cover these objects; verify grants explicitly in persistent deployments. No CREATE, table ownership, migration privilege, trigger-disabling privilege or foreign-service credential is granted to runtime. Direct reset/delete/truncate and watched-source TRUNCATE are rejected. Transaction rollback rolls back increments; a failed transaction/retry must use the existing idempotent owning workflow. No provider/HTTP I/O may hold the revision lock.

All changed rows in a watched branch invalidate all its retained days conservatively. Resolve late financial work normally, let original events reach Reporting, then capture a new reviewed generation. Revisions are not date-specific or a lock on future source commits. Source reads remain sequential observations. Monitor source transaction latency/serialization/deadlock retries during release acceptance; production contention/capacity, backup/restore and source-write/finalization policy remain gates.

Order `13→12`, Payment `12→11` and POS `10→9` refuse downgrade after any protocol-two source manifest. Use forward recovery. Empty-history downgrade/re-upgrade gets a new epoch and is covered in the disposable PostgreSQL matrix. Never remove retained history or disable triggers to force rollback. Independent backup restoration must be reviewed with source/Reporting reconciliation; an old matching revision alone is not certification.

UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE of retained manifests/audit are rejected by triggers; do not remove history to force rollback. Operators load cutoff evidence separately from preparation. Capture/refresh produces a source generation per owner and a fresh Reporting inventory check. Resolve operational work through its existing APIs, wait for retained original events to reach Reporting, then explicitly refresh. This path does not replay/repair financial sources. After interrupted work, load and resume the exact pending operation after its lease expires, or replace it with a new operation/current version. Relevant late financial recovery continues and invalidates Ready on its next authorized validation; retain the old generation as history. Ready does not authorize a settlement command.
