# Day-close cutoff evidence

This online single-branch THB foundation retains source generations and freshly reconciles sales/payment/refund identities. It does not approve or finalize settlement, stop source writes or supply a distributed cutoff. [ADR-022](../Architecture/Decisions/ADR-022-retained-day-cutoff-evidence.md) defines that boundary. Existing [day-close preparation](Day-Close-Preparation.md) remains independently available.

## Customer workflow

`GET /bff/customer/day-close-cutoffs?branchId={uuid}&businessDate=yyyy-MM-dd` loads saved cutoff evidence. `GET /bff/customer/day-close-cutoffs/csrf` returns the session antiforgery token. `POST /bff/customer/day-close-cutoffs` accepts the existing `{branchId,businessDate,operationId,expectedVersion,reasonCode}` command, with `routine_close` or `recheck`, plus `X-Nexa-CSRF`. Commands are at most 4096 bytes. Tenant, actor, bearer, calendar and UTC boundaries are derived server-side. Evidence reads and commands revalidate current subject/membership/product access.

The owning POS GET/POST route is `/api/pos/v1/customer/organizations/{organizationId}/day-close-cutoffs`. `pos.day-close.read` permits retained reads; `pos.day-close.prepare` permits capture/refresh/resume/replacement. Each source's financial read permission plus Reporting `reporting.sales.read` remains required. Accountants cannot coordinate capture. There is no new permission, financial approval or amount-limit decision.

The response reuses the preparation view: `identity`, `version`, `status`, `snapshot`, `blockers`, `validatedAtUtc`, `canPrepare`, `pendingCommand`. Status is `not_prepared`, `preparing`, `blocked` or `ready_for_review`. A completed snapshot additionally includes `cutoff`: `order`, `payment`, `pos` references, each `{manifestId,generation,evidenceVersion}`, plus `checkId`, `sourcesCurrent` and `financialGaps`. Check identities and validation times may change without changing reviewed readiness; manifest references, financial evidence, gaps or source currency may not. Customer views omit actor subjects, Authorization decisions, claim identities and audit history.

Preparing commits before remote work. Completion or invalidation commits locally with audit. Expected versions fence competing managers; a 30-second lease allows exact original-actor resume after restart. A different manager may replace expired work with a new operation/current version. Old claims cannot complete. Source captures committed before interruption remain available and exact resume reuses them. A failed refresh can complete Blocked without a snapshot. Ready GET/completed replay performs fresh checks; changed sources or outages invalidate readiness while preserving the reviewed snapshot. Blocked reads return retained evidence without claiming fresh readiness. No automatic resume or polling is supplied.

The portal labels displayed generations and financial gaps as saved evidence. Only a Ready result labels sources unchanged at this validation; blocked results require resolving blockers and refreshing. The eight-case [synthetic browser suite](../../src/Frontend/e2e/day-cutoff/README.md) verifies portal behavior with intercepted BFF replies, separately from joined identity/source-service acceptance.

`cutoff_superseded`, `cutoff_financial_gaps` and existing operational blockers prevent readiness. Unresolved orders/payments/refunds, open shifts/drawers and pending reviews independently block. Approved nonzero drawer variance remains visible. Missing/invalid manifests, absent fingerprints, inconsistent source summaries/events and unknown statuses fail closed. A new command is required to replace evidence after drift. Historical completeness recording is no longer a prerequisite for this separate cutoff check.

POS Domain requires retained cutoff references independently of the adapter. Ordinary preparation evidence without those references becomes unavailable/Blocked during cutoff completion or validation, and restoring Ready cutoff history without references is rejected.

## Owning source and Reporting routes

For `order`, `payment` and `pos`, `POST /api/{service}/v1/customer/day-cutoffs` accepts `{operationId,window:{organizationId,restaurantId,branchId,fromUtc,toUtc}}`; `GET /api/{service}/v1/customer/day-cutoffs/{manifestId}` takes the five window fields as query parameters. These retain and read evidence only, under each owner's existing day-summary financial read authority. Capture attributes the validated bearer subject; the browser cannot choose an actor. Exact operation IDs bind actor and payload and replay across process restart. Changed actor/window conflicts. Source generations are assigned per exact scoped window and are not mutation counters.

Sources accept a positive completed UTC window of at most 27 hours; POS coordination alone derives it from the completed business date and current Restaurant calendar. The response is `{manifest,current}`. A manifest contains identity/operation/generation, scope/window/capture time, fingerprint, owning summary, original `sales` or `refunds` integration contracts and bounded `ownerEvidence` JSON rows. Those rows use the existing source-owned safe selection; they contain financial identities/values, never provider secrets, tokens or arbitrary request fields. They are restricted financial evidence, not telemetry or browser storage. A source observation can be current but still have unresolved work or gaps.

`POST /api/reporting/v1/customer/day-cutoff-reconciliation` accepts `{window,orderManifestId,paymentManifestId}`. Live Reporting sales authorization and both owning source permissions apply. It reads originals, compares sales/payment/refund inventories and dedupe hashes, and rereads sources after the Reporting snapshot. Its bounded response contains check/window/manifest IDs, `sourcesCurrent`, `observed_complete`/`gaps_detected`/`sources_changed`, check time and independent expected/matched/missing/conflicting/unexpected counts. It does not record historical completeness, repair, replay events, mutate sources or authorize settlement. POS retains the result in its frozen evidence/audit and separately validates POS cash currency.

All statuses are no-store, including anonymous responses. Invalid input returns 400; authentication/permission denial 401/403; unknown scoped source manifests 404; conflicting operation IDs or POS versions/leases 409; sanitized unavailable dependencies 503. Source requests have 25-second Application/controller deadlines, owner inventory is capped at 10,000 rows/16 MiB, and the serialized retained manifest and streamed dependency body are capped at 16 MiB. Overall POS orchestration remains 25 seconds, each HTTP client 15 seconds, BFF 30 seconds and browser 40 seconds. Oversized/slow evidence cannot become Ready; caller cancellation may leave recoverable Preparing state.

## Provenance and time

Every owner captures within its own repeatable-read transaction. Source fingerprints include selected identities and financial versions; equal totals do not establish equal evidence. The original [end-of-day time bases](End-Of-Day-Draft.md) apply. Current unresolved work includes older work and is not a historical midnight reconstruction. Ready is evidence current at the checked observations only. Later recovery remains legal and requires new generations after detected drift. No cross-service snapshot, source fence, delivery watermark or provider settlement claim is made.

Query Loki by `service_name` (`nexaconnect-pos`, `nexaconnect-order`, `nexaconnect-payment`, `nexaconnect-reporting`, `nexaconnect-customer-bff`) and `|= "Day-cutoff"` or `|= "Day-close"`, then narrow by validated `CorrelationId`. New clients use shared JSON/OTLP and correlation propagation. Telemetry contains bounded status/category only; no financial bodies, amounts, retained rows, subjects, credentials or arbitrary headers are logged.
