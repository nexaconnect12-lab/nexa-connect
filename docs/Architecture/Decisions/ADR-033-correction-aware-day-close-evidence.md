# ADR-033: Correction-aware retained day-close evidence

`CashCorrectionEvidenceFingerprint` defines version-one integration encoding: EventId-sorted original identity/financial fields, normalized UTC instants and decimal scale, serialized and SHA-256 hashed. Reporting produces this value and POS independently recomputes it from retained original events before accepting delivery proof. Shared code contains encoding only; financial validation and admission remain service-owned.

Status: Accepted and implemented. Execution results are recorded separately in [acceptance evidence](../Evidence/Correction-Day-Evidence-Acceptance.md).

ADR-031 adds immutable posting-day cash variance corrections; ADR-032 supplies separate Reporting facts and live comparison. Cutoff/seal reconciliation previously proved only sale/payment/refund delivery, allowing its delivery flag to omit correction publications.

Fresh POS captures now use evidence protocol 3. In one owning repeatable-read transaction they retain the POS summary, safe owner rows, epoch/revision and verified original correction event inventory. Counts and adjustment must agree with the summary; missing or inconsistent original publication evidence fails capture. Order/Payment retain protocol 2. No source correction ledger, original tender or old settlement is changed.

Reporting cutoff and seal commands require the exact POS manifest/seal reference alongside Order and Payment. Reporting reads each owner's retained evidence under the customer's live financial authority, compares correction facts/receipt hashes, and rechecks source identities after its local comparisons. Protocol-3 results bind POS manifest/epoch/revision, sorted correction manifest hash, amount and independent expected/matched/missing/conflicting/unexpected counts. Equal totals alone do not establish delivery. The snapshots remain separate observations, not global delivery watermarks or a distributed transaction.

POS translates retained integration contracts into its own immutable correction evidence model. Independent Domain invariants block missing, conflicting, unexpected or absent proof, including an unproven empty inventory. Cutoff, seal, approval and finalization admission require it. Exact identities and adjustment follow the reviewed snapshot into the permanent settlement receipt; copies isolate item arrays. Known correction journal changes use posting-time attribution; unknown or relevant changes remain blocking. Source fences/barriers retain their existing financial guard behavior.

POS migration 22 gates fresh manifest insertion and backstops scope, inventory bound and summary. A generated nullable epoch column plus owning FK/index binds protocol-3 retention to the epoch and prevents epoch deletion while evidence exists. Old payloads remain unchanged; legacy captures replay/read but cannot admit new readiness. Retained protocol-3 history refuses downgrade. Reporting remains schema 21, with matching new binaries; Order 19 / Payment 18 / Authorization 15 remain unchanged. Compatibility is 0.36.0. No new table, permission, workload client or environment variable is added.

Historical finalized receipts and approval decisions remain readable. Legacy approval history is distinguished from a fresh candidate in the portal; fresh actions require protocol 3 and explicit correction proof. Old operation fingerprints are unchanged. In-flight undecided legacy preparations require coordinated rollout/recovery, rather than silently upgrading their reviewed snapshot. Already decided settlement recovery uses its retained decision.

Whole branch-local day coordination still accepts completed dates only. Genuine current postings can be tested through closed UTC source cuts; deterministic integration tests exercise coordinators with a controlled test calendar. This does not authorize production current-day finalization. General exports, reversal, provider/fiscal corrections, offline finalization and production certification remain separate.
